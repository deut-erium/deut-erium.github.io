Really Small Algorithm

This is a follow up to Really Simple Algorithm, If you dont understand RSA, reading my writeup may be helpful.

We are provided with n, e and ct. Recovering pt from this information is completely based on our ablitiy to factor n into primes p and q.
Now as the name is hinting towards the keyword small, one of the factors may be small. We may try factoring the given n

Alpetron is a really cool site for integer factorization, which uses ECM method for factoring integers. (I plan on writing a writeup on that)

Or the other method could be getting a list of small primes, and checking if we have a factor, as in my case.
