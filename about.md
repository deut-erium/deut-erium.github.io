I am Himanshu Sheoran, usually `deuterium` online. I work as a Security and Privacy Engineer at Google and previously worked in research and development at VMware Carbon Black. I studied Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Bombay.

Most of my spare technical work involves cryptography, reverse engineering, binary exploitation, SAT/SMT solvers, and programming language or operating-system behavior. I play CTFs with Project Sekai and zh3r0, usually on crypto tasks.
