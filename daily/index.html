How to play

  • The puzzle is a Caesar cipher: every letter was slid by the same secret shift, and everything that is not a letter (spaces, digits, punctuation) was left exactly where it stood.
  • Slide the letters back, type the decoded phrase above, and press Check. Case does not matter; extra spaces do not matter.
  • Stuck? Reveal a hint and you get exactly one letter mapping out of today's key. The share line will remember it.
  • A correct answer unlocks a celebration line and extends your streak, stored in this browser under the localStorage key daily-cipher-streak. Solve on consecutive days to grow it.
  • Answers are checked as SHA-256 digests with a per-day salt, recomputed in your browser. The plaintext answers never ship with the page, and nothing you type leaves your machine.
