Scoring rules

Both games use the same scoring rules. The daily game ends after you place 1,008 pieces, or when the blocks reach the top. Endless play has no piece limit.

Lines
1 / 2 / 3 / 4+ rows: 100 / 300 / 500 / 800
Spin Move
500 when the piece cannot move in any direction, has at least four different positions touching its blocks, has a full row through it, and did not make a square
4x4 square value
1,000 to start; +1,500 for gold; +250 for each extra piece type; +0 or 1,000 based on how the pieces fit
6x6 square value
2,500 to start; +3,500 for gold; +500 for each extra piece type; +0 to 2,500 based on how the pieces fit
8x8 square value
10,000 silver or 20,000 gold

Building a square adds no points immediately. Its row shares add up to the exact total value. Clearing a row collects its share, and earlier shares remain if a larger square forms around it. A Spin Move destroys uncollected value when it breaks the square.

Gold uses one piece type. Silver mixes piece types. Dropping faster, clearing rows on back-to-back turns, and level do not add points.
