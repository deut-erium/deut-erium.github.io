toys

Name toys

Type a name, get three things back: a tessellation tile, a melody, an avatar. Everything comes from the SHA-256 of exactly what you typed, computed in your browser, so the same name always rebuilds the same toys.

hashing your name…

Your Escher tile

A circle-limit tessellation in your palette: concentric arcs and inward triangles with four-fold symmetry, figures crowding toward the rim the way they do in Circle Limit IV.

Your melody

Sixteen bytes of the hash walked across a pentatonic scale, two octaves of room. It only makes sound when you press play; the scope underneath shows the waveform while it runs.

Your 8-bit avatar

Fifteen hashed pixels mirrored into a five-by-five grid, enlarged with crisp edges. Column three decides, columns four and five obey.
