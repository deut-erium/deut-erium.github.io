Playground

A Sudoku solver and an SMT-LIB runner using Z3 in your browser. The engine loads on first use; the page may reload.

Sudoku

Eighty-one integer variables in 1–9, pairwise distinct per row, column, and box, with your givens pinned by equality. Pick a puzzle, edit any cell, then press Solve. The grid below is the only input.

Pick a puzzle or type your own, then Solve.

SMT-LIB

Pick a preset or write an SMT-LIB 2 script, then press Run.
